Denis Makarov is the founder of DQuant and a quantitative researcher working at the intersection of financial econometrics, machine learning, and software engineering.

His work focuses on volatility forecasting and rigorous out-of-sample validation of financial models. He turns research into open tools for researchers, developers, and traders.

Denis is currently building the DQuant ecosystem and is based in Belgrade, Serbia.
